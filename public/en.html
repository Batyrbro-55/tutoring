How we notice progress

Compare what you could do at the start with what you can do now: explain a rule, solve a similar problem independently or hold a conversation. We record topics you have mastered and choose the next step.

A few more questions

Can I start if I have gaps in my knowledge?

Yes. First we identify the topics you find difficult, then build the foundations you need.

How do online lessons work?

Through video calls, worked examples and shared practice. We agree on the platform and schedule before starting.

What happens in the free introduction?

We spend 20 minutes getting acquainted, discussing your goal and trying a short task. Then we outline the next steps.

When can I join a group?

Message me on WhatsApp to discuss your level and schedule. Lessons begin once a suitable group has formed.
